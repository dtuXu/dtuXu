### 🔬 Featured — Capstone: Predicting Ankle Torque from Muscle Signals

Research capstone with McGill's REKLab (Dr. Robert Kearney) that predicts voluntary ankle torque from surface EMG.

- Stacked **LSTM** in **TensorFlow/Keras** (1,001 parameters) on 200 ms EMG windows
- EMG pipeline: Butterworth band-pass, rectification, 2 Hz envelope, normalization
- **Pooled R² ≥ 0.96** on held-out retest data for 5 of 6 subjects (best 0.991)

→ [EMG_TQ_Modelling](https://github.com/dtuXu/EMG_TQ_Modelling)
